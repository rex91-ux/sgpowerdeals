Step 1 — Open your SP utilities account

Electricity and water in a new BTO flat are already “pre-turned on”, and BTO flats don’t need a turn-on appointment. But SP’s guide for new BTO owners says to open your utilities account within 3 days of collecting your keys, or the pre-turn-on facility is withdrawn.

  • Where to applyThe SP app, the Open Utilities Account portal on spgroup.com.sg, or SP’s customer service centre. Singpass MyInfo fills in most of the form.
  • What you needNRIC or FIN, and possibly proof of ownership. You must be 18 or older.
  • Lead timeSP asks for up to 7 business days after you apply — so apply before key collection if you can.
  • GasOnce SP confirms your account, call City Energy on 1800 555 1661 to book gas activation.

Before you move in, check the meter outside and the distribution board inside. Switch on the main circuit breaker, then the residual current circuit breaker, then each miniature breaker one at a time; if one won’t stay on, tell your HDB branch office.

Step 2 — Move off SP’s tariff

Once your SP account is open you’re on the regulated tariff by default — 31.16¢/kWh for October–December 2026, and it changes every quarter. To buy from a retailer instead, sign up with them using your SP utilities account number. The retailer tells SP, and your contract can start as early as 5 business days after that. You keep one SP account for water, gas and refuse, and you keep your U-Save rebates.

New-home tips

Renovating first? A per-kWh fixed rate costs nothing while usage is low, so you can sign up as soon as the SP account is open.

Contract length: a BTO’s 5-year minimum occupation period means a 3-year plan won’t clash with a move. Early-exit charges apply if you leave sooner — Tuas, for example, charges $200 plus rebate clawback.

Aircon-heavy? Your savings scale with usage: at 600 kWh a month, 2.26¢ less per kWh is about $160 a year.

Questions

Frequently asked

How do I set up electricity for a new BTO flat?

Open an SP utilities account through the SP app or SP’s Open Utilities Account portal, ideally before key collection and no later than 3 days after. Electricity and water are pre-turned on in new BTO flats, so no appointment is needed. Then, if you want a fixed rate, sign up with an electricity retailer using your SP account number.

How much is the SP Group deposit for an HDB flat?

For Singapore Citizens and PRs, $70 with GIRO or $100 without for a 3-, 4- or 5-room HDB flat, and $40 or $60 for 1- and 2-room flats. Foreigners pay double. The deposit is charged on your first bill and refunded when the account is closed.

Can I sign up with an electricity retailer before I get my keys?

You need an SP utilities account number to sign up with a retailer, so open the SP account first. Once you have it, the retailer contract can start as early as 5 business days after the retailer informs SP.

Do I still need an SP account if I use a retailer?

Yes. Your SP utilities account stays open for water, gas and refuse charges, and SP still reads your meter. The retailer only replaces the electricity charge.

How much can a new 4-room BTO save by switching from SP?

At SP’s average of about 391 kWh a month for a 4-room flat, a 28.90¢ fixed plan costs about $106 a year less than SP’s Q4 2026 tariff of 31.16¢, before any sign-up rebate or referral credit.
