Questions

Frequently asked

Is there a Muse invite or referral code?

Yes — Muse users can share an invite code. Mine is DT74GI: join Muse, then redeem the code in Settings within 48 hours, and we each get 1 billion Muse tokens.

How do I redeem a Muse code?

Create your account at muse.ai or in the “Muse from Meta” app on iOS or Android, open Settings and enter the code. It has to be within 48 hours of joining — after that the code can’t be added.

Is Muse free in Singapore?

Meta says Muse is free for most of what people need, with subscription plans for people who want to do more. It has been available in Singapore since 27 October 2026.

Is Muse safe to connect to my accounts?

Each Muse runs on its own isolated cloud computer, which Meta calls Muse Secure VM. It keeps passwords and payment methods out of the agent’s view, you choose what it can access, and it asks before sensitive actions such as sending an email or making a purchase. You can see a log of everything it has done.

Muse or Tab — which is better?

I use Muse: it’s open to everyone in Singapore, free for most tasks, and connects to Gmail, Google Calendar and local apps like Grab and Shopee. Tab suits you if you’d rather do everything by texting on WhatsApp or iMessage; it’s in private beta, and my invite link skips the waitlist.
