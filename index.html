How the rates here are checked

Singapore's retailers reprice several times a year — Tuas has moved roughly monthly through 2026, and PacificLight raised its long plans on 20 August after holding all summer — so a referral page written once and left alone is worthless within months. Rates are read from the retailers' own plan factsheets, not from comparison blogs, and every page carries the date it was last verified. All rates are quoted including 9% GST, which is how retailers quote them; note that SP announces the regulated tariff before GST, so comparing the two directly overstates savings by 9%.
