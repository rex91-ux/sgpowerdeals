How the rates here are checked

Singapore's retailers reprice several times a year, and not always in the same direction — in October 2026 Tuas raised its fixed rates while PacificLight cut, and Senoko raised its public rates two weeks later — so a referral page written once and left alone is worthless within months. Rates are read from the retailers' own plan factsheets, not from comparison blogs, and every page carries the date it was last verified. All rates are quoted including 9% GST, which is how retailers quote them; SP announces the regulated tariff before GST, so comparing the two directly overstates savings by 9%.

Disclosure. Codes on this site are my own referral codes. When you use one you get the new-customer reward and I get the referrer reward — it costs you nothing extra and doesn't change your rate. The one exception is Keppel Electric, whose referral link opens a referral-only plan; its page says when you're better off without it. Where a retailer's own promo beats its referral code, the comparison above counts the promo.
