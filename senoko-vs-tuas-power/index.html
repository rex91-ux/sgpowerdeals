What the extra 1.68¢ buys you at Tuas

Every Tuas plan comes with 12 months of free insurance, underwritten by Singlife — you choose Home, Travel or Personal Accident cover. If you would have bought one of those anyway, it can be worth more than the $225 difference. If not, Senoko is simply the cheaper bill.

Two catches at Tuas: leaving early costs a $200 charge plus clawback of the sign-up rebate, and its referral credit applies only to PowerFIX 24 and 36. Senoko’s referral works on any plan for new customers and its Friends discount covers the LifePower and LifeSteady plans.

Questions

Frequently asked

Is Senoko or Tuas Power cheaper?

Senoko, if you sign up through a referral. Its Family & Friends rate on LifePower36 is 28.95¢/kWh against 30.63¢ on Tuas PowerFIX 36, about $225 less over three years at average 4-room usage after each retailer’s rebates (24 Oct 2026). On Senoko’s public rate of 29.95¢ the gap narrows to about $85.

What does Tuas Power offer that Senoko doesn’t?

Twelve months of free insurance on every Tuas plan — Home, Travel or Personal Accident cover, underwritten by Singlife. Senoko offers bigger promo-code rebates and gift options instead.

When does the Tuas Power online rebate end?

Tuas’s ONLINE rebate — $160 on PowerFIX 36, $100 on PowerFIX 24 — runs until 1 November 2026. After that Tuas costs about $385 more than Senoko over three years unless a new campaign replaces it.

Can I use a referral code and a promo code together?

At Senoko, yes — its referral page tells you to stack the referral code with a promotion code. At Tuas, the S$20 referral credit comes on top of the online rebate on PowerFIX 24 and 36.

Is Senoko the cheapest electricity retailer in Singapore?

Not quite. PacificLight’s Savvy Saver 36 is 28.90¢, 0.05¢ under Senoko’s Friends rate, and has a bigger rebate while its GETBACK36 code lasts. The homepage comparison ranks all four retailers at your usage.
