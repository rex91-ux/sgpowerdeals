Questions

Frequently asked

Does this Senoko Energy referral code still work?

Yes, as at 24 October 2026. Senoko referral codes belong to a customer account rather than a campaign. Enter S7RMM628 at checkout when you sign up as a new customer.

How much is the Senoko Energy referral reward?

A $20 bill rebate each for you and the person who referred you, credited within 7 working days of your contract start date. Referred sign-ups also get Senoko's Family & Friends rates, 1¢/kWh below its public fixed rates — on LifePower36 that is worth about $140 over the contract at average usage.

Can I use the referral code with a Senoko promo code?

Yes. Senoko's referral page tells you to stack the referral code with a promotion code. The promo rebates — up to $200 with a Maybank card, or $170 by GIRO — mostly require you to set up recurring payment.

Which Senoko plans can I use the referral code on?

Senoko doesn't restrict the referral to particular plans; it's for new customers signing up. The Family & Friends discount applies to the LifePower and LifeSteady plans. LifeSavvy24 and LifeGreen24 are priced the same either way.

Can existing Senoko customers use a referral code when renewing?

No. Senoko says referral codes are for new customers only and don't apply to contract renewals. Customers coming up for renewal are directed to their Senoko account for renewal rates.

Is Senoko Energy cheaper than PacificLight?

On 36 months, about the same — but only through a referral. Senoko's Friends rate on LifePower36 is 28.95¢ against PacificLight's 28.90¢, a difference of about $7 over three years. Senoko's public rate is 29.95¢. On 24 months PacificLight is cheaper: 29.00¢ against Senoko's 29.50¢ Friends rate.

Did Senoko raise its rates?

Yes. In mid-October 2026 Senoko's public LifePower36 was 28.90¢ and LifePower24 was 29.00¢. By 24 October they had risen to 29.95¢ and 30.50¢. The Family & Friends rates available through a referral are 28.95¢ and 29.50¢.

Will my U-Save rebates still apply if I switch to Senoko?

Yes. Senoko states its plans are applicable with U-Save, and if you have an outstanding bill when your referral rebate lands, Senoko applies U-Save first and then the referral rebate.
