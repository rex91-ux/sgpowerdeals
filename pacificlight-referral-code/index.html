Questions

Frequently asked

Does this PacificLight referral code still work?

Yes, as at 24 August 2026. PacificLight referral codes don't expire — they're tied to an active customer account rather than a campaign end date. If a code gets rejected it's almost always because the chosen plan isn't eligible, not because the code is dead.

How much is the PacificLight referral reward?

S$20 off your bill as the new customer, and S$30 for whoever referred you. Both are bill credits applied within 90 days of a successful sign-up and account transfer.

Where do I enter the referral code?

In the "Referral Code" field on PacificLight's online sign-up form, during checkout. It can't be added after your account is created, and customer service can't apply it retroactively.

Which plans are eligible for the referral code?

Only online sign-ups on 24, 36 or 60-month residential plans. Twelve-month plans, no-contract plans, and sign-ups made by phone or in person don't qualify.

Is PacificLight actually the cheapest electricity retailer in Singapore?

Not any more. It was until 20 August 2026 — PacificLight held its rates through early August while Senoko, Tuas and Geneco each raised theirs by about 2.2¢, then it followed on 20 August. Savvy Saver 24 is now 29.80¢, identical to Geneco, Keppel, Sembcorp, Senoko and Tuas. Savvy Saver 36 is 29.40¢, identical to Tuas and Senoko. Nobody is cheapest on rate, so compare the sign-up rebates and perks instead — and check the date on this page, because rates move several times a year.

Can I use a referral code if I'm recontracting with PacificLight?

No. The referral programme is for new customers transferring their account across. Existing customers recontracting should look at PacificLight's recontract promotions instead.

Can I combine the referral code with another promo code?

No — the referral reward can't be stacked with other offers. Compare them and use whichever is worth more. A bundled sign-up promotion is sometimes larger than S$20.

When will the S$20 appear on my bill?

Within 90 days of a successful sign-up and account transfer. It commonly skips the first bill and lands on the second or third.

What happens to the credit if I terminate early?

Any unused or excess referral credit is forfeited on early termination of supply, and your plan's early termination charges still apply on top.
