All rates on this page include 9% GST. This is the single most common way these comparisons go wrong: retailers quote their plans after GST, but SP announces the regulated tariff before GST. Compare an after-GST retailer rate against a before-GST tariff and you'll overstate your saving by 9%.

The honest trade-off

Easy Peasy looks cheapest at 27.00¢ until you notice the 55¢ daily charge. On 388.5 kWh that adds $16.50 a month — an effective 31.25¢/kWh, which is now dearer than the regulated tariff itself, let alone every fixed plan here. The same trap applies to Easy Save and, more steeply, to 9 to 9 at $1.01 a day. Savvy Saver 36 at 28.90¢ is the cheapest realistic option, but it locks you in for three years with early termination charges of $160–$2,000. If you might move house soon, S$20 is not a reason to sign a three-year contract. If you were taking a long fixed plan anyway, use the code — it's free money.

Questions

Frequently asked

Does this PacificLight referral code still work?

Yes, as at 13 October 2026. PacificLight referral codes don't expire — they're tied to an active customer account rather than a campaign end date. If a code gets rejected it's almost always because the chosen plan isn't eligible, not because the code is dead.

How much is the PacificLight referral reward?

S$20 off your bill as the new customer, and S$30 for whoever referred you. Both are bill credits applied within 90 days of a successful sign-up and account transfer.

Where do I enter the referral code?

In the "Referral Code" field on PacificLight's online sign-up form, during checkout. It can't be added after your account is created, and customer service can't apply it retroactively.

Is Pacific Light the same as PacificLight?

Yes — one company, two spellings. The retailer styles its own name as one word, PacificLight, but most people type it as two, Pacific Light, and both turn up the same electricity retailer: PacificLight Energy Pte Ltd, which has supplied Singapore's Open Electricity Market since 2018. The referral code UUXEBM4TID works regardless of how you found the page.

Worth knowing it is not connected to similarly-named businesses outside Singapore's electricity market.

Which plans are eligible for the referral code?

Only online sign-ups on 24, 36 or 60-month residential plans. Twelve-month plans, no-contract plans, and sign-ups made by phone or in person don't qualify.

Is PacificLight actually the cheapest electricity retailer in Singapore?

It is among the cheapest, but not alone. After SP's regulated tariff fell 10.4% on 1 October, PacificLight cut to 29.00¢ on 24 months and 28.90¢ on 36 — matched by Geneco on 24, while Senoko's referral-only rate is 28.95¢ on 36 and its public rates have risen to 29.95¢ and 30.50¢. Tuas moved the other way, to 30.90¢ and 30.63¢, so PacificLight beats Tuas by about 1.7¢ but ties Geneco on 24 months. Keppel Electric is higher, at 30.90¢ on its public 24-month plan, and Sembcorp does not publish a rate publicly. So compare the sign-up rebates and perks between the three that tie — and check the date on this page, because rates move several times a year.

Can I use a referral code if I'm recontracting with PacificLight?

No. The referral programme is for new customers transferring their account across. Existing customers recontracting should look at PacificLight's recontract promotions instead.

Can I combine the referral code with another promo code?

No — the referral reward can't be stacked with other offers. Compare them and use whichever is worth more. A bundled sign-up promotion is sometimes larger than S$20.

When will the S$20 appear on my bill?

Within 90 days of a successful sign-up and account transfer. It commonly skips the first bill and lands on the second or third.

What happens to the credit if I terminate early?

Any unused or excess referral credit is forfeited on early termination of supply, and your plan's early termination charges still apply on top.
