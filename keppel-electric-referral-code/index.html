Four steps

How the Keppel referral works

  1. Open the link

    It goes straight to Keppel's sign-up page with FIXED24 Special already selected. It's the only plan the link offers.

  2. Don't type a code

    The link applies Keppel's referral promo code, REFER001, by itself. That code is the same for every referrer — typing it by hand on another plan won't get you the referral rate or the $25.

  3. Add a Maybank card if you have one

    Setting up recurring payment on a Maybank credit card adds a $20 rebate on top, so the most the link pays is $45. That promotion runs 1 October to 31 December 2026 and is limited to the first 125 sign-ups.

  4. Look for $25 on your first bill

    Keppel credits the new customer's $25 on the first bill after the contract starts. The referrer's $25 is paid once your contract has started.

Who can't use it

The referral is for new sign-ups only. Keppel's terms exclude recontracts and relocations, so existing Keppel customers renewing or moving house won't get it. There is no 36-month referral: Keppel advertises the referral on FIXED24 Special only, and no referral link for FIXED36 or any other plan appears anywhere public.

A wording mismatch worth knowing about: Keppel's referral page says the reward applies to "any new sign up of 2-year electricity plan", which would include the public FIXED24. But the referral terms name FIXED24 Special specifically, and the link itself only offers that plan. Go by the link.

Questions

Frequently asked

Is there a Keppel Electric referral code?

Not one you type in. Keppel's referral works through a personal link that each customer gets from the Keppel Customer Portal. The link applies the promo code REFER001 automatically, but REFER001 is the same for every referrer, so typing it by hand doesn't credit anyone or unlock the referral plan. Use the link.

Does this Keppel referral link still work?

Yes, as at 24 October 2026. The link opens Keppel's FIXED24 Special plan at 30.40¢/kWh including GST, with the $25 referral rebate shown on the sign-up page.

How much is the Keppel Electric referral reward?

$25 for the new customer, credited on the first bill after the contract starts, and $25 in referral credits for the person who referred you once your contract has started. A Maybank card rebate of $20 can be added on top, for up to $45.

Which Keppel plans can I use the referral on?

Only FIXED24 Special, the 24-month plan the referral link opens. It is a new-sign-up offer and doesn't apply to recontracts or relocations. Keppel doesn't advertise a referral on FIXED36 or any other plan.

Can I combine the referral with NEW125?

No. NEW125 is the $125 rebate on Keppel's public FIXED24 at 30.90¢; the referral link puts you on FIXED24 Special at 30.40¢ with $25. They're separate plans, so it's one or the other. For an average household using 388.5 kWh a month, NEW125 is about $53 cheaper over 24 months. The referral link only comes out ahead above roughly 833 kWh a month.

Is Keppel Electric cheaper than PacificLight?

No. Keppel's public 24-month rate is 30.90¢ and its referral-only rate is 30.40¢, both above the 29.00¢ that PacificLight and Geneco charge on 24 months. Keppel's public FIXED24, FIXED36 and FIXED12 rates match Tuas Power's exactly.

Will my U-Save rebates still apply if I switch to Keppel Electric?

Yes. U-Save and GST Voucher rebates are applied through your SP Group utilities account, which stays with SP when you switch retailer, so they keep offsetting your bill. This is true of any Open Electricity Market retailer.
